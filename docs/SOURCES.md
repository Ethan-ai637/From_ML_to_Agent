# Research Frontier source policy

本教材中的 2024--2026 Research Frontier 卡片优先使用会议官方 Awards、Virtual Program、Proceedings 或 ACL Anthology 核验身份与摘要。

主要官方入口：
- ICLR 2026 Outstanding Papers: https://blog.iclr.cc/2026/04/23/announcing-the-iclr-2026-outstanding-papers/
- ICLR 2025 Outstanding Papers: https://blog.iclr.cc/2025/04/22/announcing-the-outstanding-paper-awards-at-iclr-2025/
- ICML 2026 Awards: https://blog.icml.cc/2026/07/05/announcing-the-icml-2026-awards/
- ICML 2024 Awards: https://icml.cc/virtual/2024/awards_detail
- NeurIPS 2025 Best Paper Awards: https://blog.neurips.cc/2025/11/26/announcing-the-neurips-2025-best-paper-awards/
- ACL 2025 Awards: https://2025.aclweb.org/program/awards/

重点核验入口还包括 ICLR 2026 Oral program、官方 ICLR proceedings、ACL Anthology，以及 The Coverage Principle、Quantitative Bounds for Length Generalization、Sequences of Logits Reveal the Low Rank Structure of Language Models、RLVR Implicitly Incentivizes Correct Reasoning in Base LLMs、The Polar Express、Native Sparse Attention、Between Circuits and Chomsky、Language Models Resist Alignment 等论文的官方页面。

Evidence policy: Frontier cards should use attribution language for empirical results. Conference award/oral status establishes selection/relevance, not universal truth; mechanism-level labs do not substitute for paper-scale replication.
